Voice commands

Experimental — short taps

Speak a phrase to send a quick tap to the game. Voice does not hold throttle or steering continuously. Keep this tab open, allow the microphone, and keep the game focused while driving.

Idle

Try saying

  • “turn left” or “left” — short steer left
  • “turn right” or “right” — short steer right
  • “go” / “accelerate” — short throttle tap
  • “stop” / “brake” — short brake tap
  • “boost” — short boost tap
  • “pause” — open pause menu
