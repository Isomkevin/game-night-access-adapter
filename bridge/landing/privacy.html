Privacy Policy

Game Night Access Adapter · Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers the Game Night Access Adapter Chrome extension and the related local bridge / desktop app. Plain language is intentional.

What the product does

It remaps accessibility inputs (phone tilt, camera/head gestures, voice, on-screen buttons) onto keyboard actions so you can play keyboard-driven browser games. You choose which game tab receives those keys with Use this tab.

What we collect

We do not run a Game Night account system. We do not sell personal data.

  • Remap settings (which input maps to which key) are stored in Chrome’s local extension storage on your computer (storage permission).
  • Camera frames are processed on your device for gestures. They are not uploaded to Game Night servers.
  • Phone controller motion and button events go only to the access bridge you are using (usually on your own computer at localhost, or a bridge URL you chose).
  • Voice: when you turn listening on, Chrome’s built-in speech recognition may send audio to Google while listening is active (Chrome’s behaviour). The extension does not store audio or transcripts. Microphone permission is requested by the page or Chrome prompt when you use voice.

Permissions we ask for

  • storage — remember your control mappings and setup progress.
  • tabs / activeTab — find and pin the game tab you want to control.
  • Host access to http(s) pages — games can run on many websites; the extension must be able to send keyboard events into the tab you pinned. We do not scrape or upload page content for advertising.

Bridge and desktop app

The local bridge (including the Game Night desktop tray app) runs on your machine and serves controller pages. Network traffic for controllers stays on your LAN or the bridge host you configured. The optional hosted demo bridge on Render is only used if you point the extension at that address.

Children

The product is a general accessibility tool for browser games. Guardians should supervise younger players’ browser and microphone use.
